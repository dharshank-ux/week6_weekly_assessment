COMPANY POLICY

Section 1: Working Hours

Employees must work 8 hours per day.
Office working hours are from 9 AM to 6 PM.
Employees must inform their manager if they arrive late.

Section 2: Attendance

All employees must mark attendance daily.
Employees should inform HR about attendance issues.
Repeated absence without permission may result in disciplinary action.

Section 3: Workplace Rules

Employees must maintain professional behavior.
Company resources should be used responsibly.
Employees must follow company rules and regulations.
