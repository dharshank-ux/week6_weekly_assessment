EMPLOYEE MANUAL

Section 1: Employee Responsibilities

Employees must complete their assigned tasks on time.
Employees should communicate clearly with team members.
Employees must maintain work quality and productivity.

Section 2: Communication

Employees should use official email for company communication.
Important updates must be shared with the team.
Employees should attend scheduled team meetings.

Section 3: Performance

Employee performance will be reviewed regularly.
Managers will provide feedback and guidance.
Employees are encouraged to improve their technical skills.
