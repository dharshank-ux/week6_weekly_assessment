LEAVE POLICY

Section 1: Annual Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 days of annual leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted in advance.
Managers must approve annual leave requests.

Section 2: Sick Leave

Employees can apply for sick leave when they are unwell.
Medical certificates may be required for extended sick leave.
Employees must inform their manager about sick leave.

Section 3: Leave Approval

All leave requests must be submitted through HR.
Employees should check their leave balance before applying.
Unapproved leave may be treated as unauthorized absence.
