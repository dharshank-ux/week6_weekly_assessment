HR GUIDELINES

Section 1: Recruitment

All job applicants must complete the interview process.
HR will verify candidate qualifications.
Selected candidates will receive an offer letter.

Section 2: Employee Onboarding

New employees must complete onboarding training.
HR will explain company policies and procedures.
Employees must submit required joining documents.

Section 3: Employee Benefits

Employees are eligible for company benefits.
HR will provide information about insurance and benefits.
Employees can contact HR for benefit-related questions.
